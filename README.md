2. PeerGuide : AI-Based Student Guidance and Peer Recommendation Platform

Problem Statement: Students often struggle to find proper academic and future guidance due to random and unstructured online interactions, making it difficult to receive relevant and reliable support.

Proposed Solution: The project proposes a guided platform that recommends suitable peers or seniors based on academic level, field of study, and learning goals to enable meaningful guidance.

Approach: The system uses user profiles and a rule-based or AI-assisted matching mechanism, supported by cloud-based storage and deployment, in which student gets recommended peers and seniors or someone pursuing higher studies based on their interest,field of study, academic goals. there students can connect with them in chats , voice call or video call..and can get the required guidance or help.

Expected Impact: The platform aims to improve academic clarity, encourage structured peer learning, and reduce the guidance gap among students.
